SANJI

West African Roots · Modern Fire

About

Born from the Doso hunter's tradition of West Africa, the N'goni carries a healing resonance that has moved people for centuries. SANJI brings these ancient instruments into a contemporary American context — five musicians channeling the deep-rooted power of the Kamale N'goni, djembe, and voice into something visceral, danceable, and alive.
