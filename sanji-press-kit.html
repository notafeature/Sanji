About

Born from the Donso hunter's tradition of West Africa, the kamale n'goni carries a healing resonance that has moved people for centuries. SANJI brings these ancient instruments into a contemporary context — five musicians channeling the deep-rooted power of the Kamale N'goni, djembe, and voice into something visceral and alive.
